A 1080P animated short film built entirely through code (Remotion, React, Three.js, and Blender).

- **Theme & Motif**: "Idea is all we need" — computing power is an amplifier for human imagination, not its replacement.
- **Visual Progression**: Color palettes unlock with human warmth rather than technological eras, starting with 1-bit monochrome in the Stone Age, warm ochre campfire cave art, full mineral-pigment ancient civilizations, retro pixel computing, and culminating in luminous particle forests and starship launches before returning to black-and-white.
