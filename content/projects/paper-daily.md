An autonomous literature intelligence workbench built for researchers to streamline literature discovery, hypothesis verification, and automated paper reading.

- **4-Layer DeepResearch Loop**: Orchestrates arXiv, DBLP, OpenReview, and Semantic Scholar scrapers with multi-source ranking and LLM-guided relevance filtering.
- **Active Hypothesis Verification**: State-machine driven claim extraction and counter-evidence retrieval directly linking back to source citations.
- **In-Situ PDF Annotations**: Automatic 3-color highlight generation directly on source research paper PDFs.
- **Cross-Platform Knowledge Sync**: Bi-directional integration connecting Zotero, Feishu/Lark, Telegram, and WeChat Daily Broadcasts.
- **Serverless Architecture**: 100% serverless deployment pipeline orchestrated on Cloudflare Workers & Pages.
